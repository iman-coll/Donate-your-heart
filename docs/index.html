🕌 Zakat Calculator

      
Work out 2.5% of the wealth you have held for one lunar year, once it is above the nisaab.

    


    

      

      

      

      

      

      


      

        How do I work out the nisaab?
        
The nisaab is the threshold below which no zakat is due. The two classical measures are
          87.48 g of gold and 612.36 g of silver. Many scholars prefer the silver
          figure, because it is lower and so more people qualify to give.

        

          

            

          

        

        
        
You enter the metal rate yourself, because this app makes no network requests at all —
          so it cannot fetch a live gold or silver price, and a stale price would be worse than none.

      


      

      
Nisaab is the line between owing zakat and not. Leave it blank and you will still see your total wealth.

📖 Donation Guide

      
What to give, what to leave out, and how to hand it over so it actually reaches someone.

    


    
✅ Before you give

    

      
Wash it and let it dryDamp clothes grow mildew in a closed bag and the whole bag gets thrown away.

      
Sort, don't mixOne bag per category beats one bag of everything. Sorting is the charity's single biggest cost.

      
Check pockets, zips and cordsEmpty them, and pack the charger with the device it belongs to.

      
Give what you would hand to a friendIf you would apologise while handing it over, it needs recycling, not a donation.

    


    
💗 What travels well

    

      
🧥 Clothes, all seasons, all agesThe biggest need by far, and the easiest to get wrong — clean and dry is what matters.

      
📚 Textbooks, storybooks, notebooksSchool sets are always short. Tear out nothing; a complete set is worth ten loose books.

      
🎒 School bags and stationeryPencils, copies, geometry boxes and bags go straight to students before term.

      
🍚 Sealed, in-date packaged foodFlour, rice, lentils, oil, tea, milk powder, dates. Check the date yourself.

      
🧸 Toys and games, completeA puzzle with the pieces, a bear that can be washed. Small and complete beats big and broken.

      
🛏️ Blankets and quilts, in winterThe most requested item in December and January, and the least donated.

      
🔌 Working small appliancesFans, irons, kettles, radios — with the cable. Wipe it down and test it first.

      
🩸 Ration-bag itemsRice, sugar, oil and tea in sealed packs are what a rashan bag is actually made of.

    


    
🚫 What charities usually cannot take

    

      
Soiled, torn or wet clothingNobody can repair it at scale. It costs the charity money to dispose of.

      
Used underwear and socksUnless brand new and still sealed. This is the rule most people break.

      
Expired or opened foodAlso anything in a damaged or unlabelled packet.

      
Loose or unlabelled medicinesOnly sealed, in-date, pharmacy-readable packs — and ask first, because most sites are not licensed to take them.

      
Broken or incomplete electronicsNo cracked screens, no missing parts, no CRT televisions.

      
Medical wasteSyringes, dressings, expired IV supplies. This is hazardous waste, not a donation.

      
Furniture needing repairUnless the site has agreed to collect it and has the space and a workshop.

      
Anything you would apologise forThe clearest test there is. When in doubt, ask the site first with a photo.

    

    
Every organisation sets its own rules, and this is general guidance — not a policy statement
      from any charity. Call the site before you load the car, especially for anything large, electrical
      or medical.


    
🏷️ The condition grades in this app

    

      
Like NewUnused or used once. Fits on a shelf in a shop.

      
GoodClean, complete, works. Someone would happily buy it second-hand.

      
UsableShows wear but functions. Honest to declare, and still wanted.

      
Needs RepairOnly if you know the site repairs things. Ask first — many cannot.

    


    
📦 Getting it there

    

      
1 · Pick a siteUse the Sites tab, filter to your city, and check what they accept.

      
2 · Call aheadConfirm opening hours and that they still want what you are bringing. Numbers change.

      
3 · Make a ticketThe Donate tab turns your list into a printable ticket with the site's address on it.

      
4 · Ask for a receiptIf you want a record, ask for one at the counter. A bank trail is safer than cash.

    

    
🚚 Can't travel? TCS, Leopards and Bykea will deliver a parcel to the charity's published
      address. Call the charity first so they expect it, write the contents on the outside, and keep the courier
      receipt. Ask before you send anything electrical or medical.


    
💗 Give things people need, not things you want gone. The point of a donation is the
      person receiving it — that is also why this app uses an illustration rather than a photograph of a child.

🔍 How to spot a fake appeal

    

      
A personal account numberBanks in Pakistan are not allowed to let personal accounts collect donations — the account title must match the organisation. A JazzCash or Easypaisa number in someone's own name is the single biggest red flag.

      
A look-alike website or pageEdhi's own homepage carries a permanent warning about copycat domains. Type the organisation's address yourself instead of tapping a forwarded link.

      
No registration, address or landlineA real organisation can show a registration certificate and a physical office. Ask for both.

      
Urgency plus a deadline“Only today”, “last 3 trucks”, a countdown. Real appeals do not expire in an hour.

      
Photos you cannot placeOld images get recycled between disasters. A reverse image search takes ten seconds.

      
“Forward this to 10 people”Chain-message appeals almost never have a verifiable recipient behind them.

    


    
✅ Check before you give

    

      
PCP NPO DirectoryPakistan Centre for Philanthropy lists organisations and the years it evaluated them. pcp.org.pk/npo-directory ↗

      
Sindh Charity CommissionPublic charity search, plus the list of proscribed organisations. charitycommission.sindh.gov.pk ↗

      
Call the national lineEdhi 115, Chhipa 1020 and Saylani 111-729-526 all answer in every city and can confirm whether an appeal is theirs.

    


    
This app never collects, holds or transfers money. It only points you at an
      organisation's own published contact details. If a page asks you to pay this app, it is not this app.

  


  
  

    

      
ℹ️ About & Trust

      
Who made this, what it does and does not do, and how to check it yourself.

    


    

      
An independent directoryIman Donation Trust is a student-built prototype that helps you find somewhere to take things you want to give away. It is not affiliated with, and not endorsed by, any organisation listed here — Edhi, Chhipa, Saylani, Al-Khidmat, Shaukat Khanum and the rest are named only so you can contact them.

      
It does not collect moneyThere is no payment, no wallet, no bank account and no donation form anywhere in this app. It links to organisations' own published phone numbers.

      
You can run it offlineOne HTML file. No accounts, no analytics, no cookies, no network requests after the page loads.

    


    
🔎 How the directory is built — and its limits

    

      
Compiled from public sourcesAddresses, hours and categories were taken from organisations' own websites and public listings. They are not individually verified by us.

      
Helplines carry a sourceFour of the five national numbers link to the page they came from and the date they were checked, so a wrong number is visible rather than hidden. Chhipa's was 1121 here once; it is 1020, and that is exactly the kind of error the source links exist to catch.

      
“Accepts everything” means “ask them”Where a site is marked as taking any category, that is a prompt to call, not a promise from the charity.

      
Not every genuine charity is certifiedMany small, real organisations have no PCP evaluation. “No record found” is not the same as “not real” — but a personal account number always is a problem.

    


    
🧾 Check an organisation yourself

    

      
Pakistan Centre for PhilanthropyFBR's designated certification agency, with evaluation years per organisation. pcp.org.pk/npo-directory ↗

      
Sindh Charity CommissionPublic charity search and proscribed-organisation list. charitycommission.sindh.gov.pk ↗

      
Punjab and KP charity commissionsBoth run registration portals and publish guidance for donors.

      
Ask for the certificateAny real organisation can show its registration certificate and a governing-body resolution, and will not mind being asked.

      
Never trust a personal accountBanks in Pakistan may not let personal accounts collect donations, and an advertised donation account must be titled in the organisation's own name.

    


    
🔒 Your data

    

      
Nothing is uploadedYour name, phone number, basket and donation history are stored only in this browser on this device. There is no server to send them to.

      
What “Export my data” containsEverything above, as a JSON file — including your name and phone number, so keep the file somewhere you would keep a receipt.

      
“Clear everything” means itOn the Profile tab. It wipes the basket, the history, the profile and this app's saved settings.

      
One caveat worth knowingOn GitHub Pages this page shares an origin with every other project under the same account, so another page on that account could in principle read this browser's stored data. It is not sent anywhere — but if you would rather not store your phone number, use the ticket without saving it.

    


    
✏️ Something wrong? Tell us

    

      
Wrong number or closed branch?Open an issue at github.com/iman-coll/Donate-your-heart/issues ↗ with the site name and what you found. Please include the source you checked.

      
An organisation that should be listed?Same place. A published address and a phone number from the organisation's own site is what we need.

    


    
Add a correction date and a source to every listing you change, and the directory stays
      honest as it grows. That habit matters more than the number of sites in it.
